# Service Agreement Review Checklist

The current service agreement template is based on the supplied 9-page sample
agreement structure. It is not approved production legal wording.

Before production, the client must review and approve:

- [ ] Business legal name and operating name
- [ ] Consultant name, title, licence number, and credential wording
- [ ] Business address, email, phone, and website
- [ ] CICC membership wording, complaint link, and CICC contact details
- [ ] Authorization clause and representative appointment wording
- [ ] Pre-conditions, admissibility, and expert-referral wording
- [ ] Processing-time language and official-source references
- [ ] Client instructions and application-type wording
- [ ] Client declaration, misrepresentation, communication, and response-time wording
- [ ] Licensee declaration wording
- [ ] Scope of professional services for each application type
- [ ] Timeline, responsibility, and fee table
- [ ] Professional fees, taxes, initial payment, remaining payment, and milestones
- [ ] Payment, refund, late-payment, interest, collection-cost, and court-cost terms
- [ ] Government-fee and disbursement wording
- [ ] Confidentiality, electronic communication, and document-storage wording
- [ ] Licensee-not-responsible clause
- [ ] Force majeure clause
- [ ] Termination, withdrawal, and file-return wording
- [ ] Planned/unplanned absence wording
- [ ] Agreement and dispute-resolution wording
- [ ] Governing province and court venue
- [ ] Document-format, file-size, translation, and naming instructions
- [ ] Validation wording
- [ ] Client initials areas
- [ ] Client and licensee signature blocks
- [ ] Future electronic-signature workflow and storage policy

Do not enable production agreement delivery until this checklist is complete.
